HOA Violation Letters: Tone, CC&Rs References, and Clarity

Stay professional and clear. Reference relevant CC&Rs, provide grace periods, and outline next steps. Keep consistent wording across notices.

Tone & Structure

  • Professional and neutral tone; avoid accusatory language.
  • Clear subject and salutation; friendly closing.
  • State issue, reference CC&Rs section(s), provide remedy and timeline.
  • Offer contact for questions; outline escalation if necessary.

Sample Letter

Date: 11/07/2025

Dear Homeowner,

We are writing regarding a violation observed: Trash bins visible from the street.
Per CC&Rs Section 4.3, bins must be stored out of public view.
Please address this within 7 days to remain compliant.

Sincerely,
HOA Board
