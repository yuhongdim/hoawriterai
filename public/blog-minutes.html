How to Write HOA Meeting Minutes That Stand Up to Review

Structure agenda, capture motions/decisions, and assign action items with owners and due dates.

Why Minutes Matter

Minutes are the official record. Clear structure and consistency make them reliable references during audits, disputes, or future planning.

Best Practices

  • Follow the agenda order and use clear section headers.
  • Record motions verbatim with proposer, seconder, and vote result.
  • Summarize discussions; avoid subjective commentary.
  • List action items with owner and due date; track status next meeting.
  • Use consistent formatting and a standard template.

Sample Structure

Meeting: HOA Board — Nov 7, 2025
Location: Clubhouse

1. Call to Order (7:00pm)
2. Approval of Prior Minutes (Motion: Smith; Second: Lee; Passed 4-1)
3. Reports — Treasurer: Budget variance -2.1%; Landscaping: contract renewal pending
4. Old Business — Parking signage proposal tabled
5. New Business — Holiday lighting budget approved ($1,500)
6. Action Items — Lee: vendor quotes by Nov 21; Chen: newsletter draft by Nov 15
7. Adjournment (8:12pm)
